Our purpose

Growing food, knowledge and a better future.

More than a planting kit — GrowKit PH is a life-skill starter kit, one plant at a time.

01 — MISSION

Learning that grows with them.

To provide fun and educational grow kits that teach kids responsibility, spark curiosity about food and nature, and support parents in building healthy habits at home.

02 — VISION

Tomorrow's eco-aware leaders.

To inspire Filipino youth to grow their own food and become responsible, health-minded, and eco-aware future leaders.

Why parents love it

Kids learn best by doing.

Growing a plant builds the quiet skills that last a lifetime — and gives families something real to share.

Patience

Daily care and waiting for that first sprout teaches that good things take time.

Responsibility

Their plant depends on them — watering, sunlight and checking in every day.

Curiosity

Science and nature awareness, learned hands-on instead of from a textbook.

Healthy habits

Kids who grow their own food understand where it comes from — and want to eat it.

Confidence

Nothing beats the pride of saying, “I grew this!”

Homes & schools

Perfect for family bonding, classroom projects and productive screen-free time.

How it works

From seed to harvest.

Everything you need comes in the kit. Three simple stages, one proud little gardener.

Step 01

Plant

Fill the pot with soil, press in the seeds and give them their first drink of water.

Step 02

Care

Water, sunlight and a daily check-in. Watch the first sprout break through.

Step 03

Harvest

Pick, taste and share what you grew — and proudly say, “I grew this!”
